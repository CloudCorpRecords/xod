Tier 7 is MIXED: recurring work exists and operates, but silent stalls and duplicate or suppressed reporting are known failure modes. Tier 8 is UNSUPPORTED on the snapshot: partial fleet coordination occurs, but a mandatory shared state property is absent and contention has been observed.
