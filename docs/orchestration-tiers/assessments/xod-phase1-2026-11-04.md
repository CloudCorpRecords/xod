## Assessment basis

- **System:** Xod orchestrator, Phase 1 build.
- **Version or configuration:** Phase 1 test configuration; precise build identifier UNKNOWN.
- **Environment:** Controlled unit and live test environment described by the snapshot. Detailed device and service configuration UNKNOWN.
- **Interval:** Snapshot dated 2026-11-04.
- **Assessor:** Repository seed assessment.
- **Rubric:** Initial repository model, 2026-11-04.
- **Evidence packet:** No raw logs, test harness, run index, or destination artifacts are included in this repository. This assessment is based only on the verified snapshot facts listed below.
- **Snapshot source:** Unit suites passed three times, including a new parallel research fix recorded as 5 of 5 on each of three runs. Live pass 1 was clean at 114 of 114 checks. Live pass 2 was 113 of 114 checks. In live pass 2, a three task load test produced separate answers without leakage, chat responses were approximately 1.3 to 1.9 seconds, and the server froze once for 3.27 seconds against a 1 second limit. Parallel research was 7 of 7 in both live passes. The snapshot reports six of nine tiers proven, with the freeze gating the seventh. Tier 7 requires a real week of unattended running. Tier 8 requires real phones.
- **Material exclusions:** This is a dated snapshot of one build during one test count. It is not a permanent product claim, a release judgment, or a prediction.

## Executive result

The source snapshot reports **six of nine tiers proven**. Applying this repository's evidence standard conservatively, and because the raw packet is not included here, this seed assessment records Tiers 0 through 5 as **SUPPORTED** rather than independently PROVEN.

Tier 6 is **MIXED**. Two live passes provide substantial evidence of multi task behavior and responsiveness in most checks, but the 3.27 second freeze breaches the stated responsiveness limit and the snapshot does not by itself establish the full Tier 6 protocol of restart, steering, durable resume, and side effect accounting.

Tier 7 is **UNKNOWN** because the defining observation period, a real week of unattended recurring work, has not elapsed in the snapshot. Tier 8 is **UNKNOWN** because real phone participation required by the snapshot's own framing is not included.
