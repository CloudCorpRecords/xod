## Proposition under synthesis

Should CLM-015 to CLM-020 be entered in the claim registry, and under what qualifications?

## Current conclusion

Yes. All six are registered with status `proposed` in `claims/CLAIMS.md` and `claims/claims.yaml`, effective with this synthesis. Two qualifications from the skeptical review are adopted:

1. **CLM-018 narrowing requirement.** Before CLM-018 can move above `proposed`, a linked note must operationalize "identifiable knee" with predeclared thresholds (the skeptic's example: accuracy gains under 2 percentage points per doubling of retained content, sustained across two doublings, in a named corpus class). The registered statement itself is unchanged.
2. **CLM-016 handling procedure.** Canary statements used to test CLM-016 must be contentless with respect to real persons and decisions, timestamped and sealed before disclosure, single use, and run under the canary procedure in `docs/PROTOCOLS.md` (global procedures). A canary result obtained without the procedure is Class E evidence for the claim: present, but unable to carry a status change.

A third, documentation level rule is adopted from the review: any quotation of a registry claim outside this repository must carry its status word. This synthesis states the rule; enforcement is a matter of citation hygiene in each assessment.

## Decisive arguments

Registration asserts only that a statement is narrow, dated, and reviewable. By that standard, refusing registration to statements that openly name their refutation conditions would leave the registry able to record only settled matters, which inverts its purpose. The skeptic's two defects are real but attach to future status changes and field procedure, not to registrability today, and both are fully handled by qualifications that preserve every original word. The dilution risk is genuine and is answered by status carrying citation, the only remedy compatible with an open registry.

## Agreed facts

- None of the six statements has direct supporting evidence in this repository today.
- Each statement names evidence that would support it and evidence that would refute it.
- CLM-018 requires operationalization before any status change.
- CLM-016 requires a handling procedure before any field test can count.

## Dissent preserved

No dissent remains on registration itself. The skeptic's concern about registry dilution by fragment quotation stands as a recorded caution rather than an objection, and is the reason for the status carrying citation rule above. If future assessments are found quoting proposed claims as settled, that finding should reopen this chain under the verdict change rules (evidence reclassification).

## Current verdicts

- **Registration of CLM-015 to CLM-020 as proposed:** ADOPTED.
- **Truth of any of the six statements:** NOT ASSESSED. All six remain at confidence as registered (MODERATE except CLM-018 at LOW).

## Evidence that could change this synthesis

A demonstration that a proposed claim cannot, even in principle, be scored (for example, if CLM-015's three dimensions prove inseparable in every scoring attempt) would move that claim to a narrowing deliberation. New empirical evidence moves individual claims through their own review conditions; it does not reopen registration unless it shows the statement was never falsifiable.
