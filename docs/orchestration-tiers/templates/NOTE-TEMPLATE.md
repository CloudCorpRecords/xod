# Note Template

Copy this template into the relevant deliberation directory. Do not edit another contributor's note after submission. Corrections belong in a new note.

```markdown
# <DELIB-NNNN-NN>: <Short descriptive title>

- **Deliberation:** DELIB-<NNNN>
- **Date:** <YYYY-MM-DD>
- **Contributor:** <name, organization, model, or stable pseudonym>
- **Role:** <proposer, reviewer, skeptic, synthesizer, or evidence steward>
- **Status:** <open, answered, superseded by a later note, or synthesis candidate>
- **Claims addressed:** <CLM identifiers or NONE>
- **Prior notes addressed:** <note identifiers or NONE>
- **Assessments addressed:** <paths or NONE>

## Proposition

<State one proposition narrowly enough that evidence could support or refute it.>

## Scope

<State the system, configuration, environment, interval, tier, and exclusions. Write UNKNOWN where an element is not known.>

## Evidence inspected directly

| Evidence | Class | What it establishes | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <artifact, run, log, or read back> | <A, B, C, D, or E> | <specific proposition> | <freshness, independence, or completeness limit> |

## Evidence not inspected

<List material that exists or may exist but was not available to this contributor. Do not imply that uninspected material supports the proposition.>

## Reasoning

<Explain the path from evidence to conclusion. Cite the model and tier criteria by file and section. Separate facts, inferences, and judgments.>

## Counterarguments considered

<Name the strongest alternative interpretation and explain whether the evidence rules it out, weakens it, or leaves it open.>

## Uncertainty

<State what remains unknown and what observation would materially change the conclusion.>

## Conclusion

<Use a model verdict where appropriate, or state that the note reaches no verdict.>

## Requested next step

<Name a specific evidence request, review, rubric clarification, or synthesis action. Do not turn this field into a product task.>
```

## Filing rules

1. Assign the next sequential note identifier in the deliberation.
2. Link every prior note that the new note supports, challenges, extends, or corrects.
3. Preserve the original note unchanged after submission.
4. Use a new note for substantive corrections.
5. Include failed and unknown evidence rather than writing only the favorable path.
