# Stir Brief Template

Copy this file to open a stir. Register the stir in docs/STIR-LOG.md first; the stir id below comes from the log.

---

**Stir id:** STIR-NNNN

**Date opened:** YYYY-MM-DD

**Stirrer:** (name or agent id; must not be the target's author or assessor)

**Target:** (claim id, assessment file, or verdict under stir)

**Target author / assessor:** (name or agent id)

**Selection rule:** (which rule from STIR-PROTOCOL.md picked this target: thin support, aged evidence, unchallenged, small denominators, convenient results)

**Move level:** (Needle, Shove, Storm, Quake; one brief per move, this brief states its level)

## The soft spot

The exact weakness this stir presses on, in at most three sentences. Name the packet, the date, the N, or the missing challenge. No general unease.

## The contrary case

The strongest honest argument that the verdict is wrong, stale, or wider than its evidence. Written at full strength. If the Stirrer cannot write this section convincingly, the stir does not open.

## Evidence demanded

What fresh packet would settle it, scoped to the soft spot. Reference templates/EVIDENCE-PACKET-TEMPLATE.md sections by name.

## Storm scenario (Storm moves only)

The adversarial variation or failure injection, stated precisely enough to rerun: setup, injection point, what the claim predicts, what would count as failure under SCORECARDS.md.

## Close criteria

What result closes this move: an answer with evidence, a concession, a packet, a score. Stated before the move runs, not after.

## Synthesis (filled at close)

**Outcome:** (RE-AFFIRMED, DOWNGRADED, AMENDED, STIRRER'S MISS)

**Evidence produced:** (links to packets, issues, deliberations)

**Verdict effect:** (none, changed to X, annotated stale, claim amended as follows)
